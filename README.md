One line, 200 characters max: a single joke that lands on its own — no setup, no sequel, just the laugh.

## Entry

"I told my smart fridge a joke — now it only opens for people with a sense of humor, and my leftovers have tenure."

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets, no dependencies.
